# Logo Generation Prompt — DrishtiSport AI

## Why this design direction

Since you'll manually stamp this logo at the **bottom-right corner of every slide**, it must survive being shrunk small, and it must read clearly on **both** your dark graphite title/closing slides and your white content slides (2-8). That means: request **two versions** in the same prompt (full-color-on-transparent, and a pure white monochrome-on-transparent), and keep the icon simple enough to recognize at stamp size — no fine detail, no thin hairlines that vanish when scaled down.

The concept ties together: "Drishti" (Sanskrit/Hindi for vision/sight/insight) + a skeletal pose-tracking joint-node (your actual product's core visual, from the app itself) + a hint of athletic motion. This keeps the logo literal to your product instead of a generic "sports + AI" clip-art combo.

---

## THE PROMPT (paste into ChatGPT / Gemini / DALL-E)

```
Design a modern, minimal tech logo for "DrishtiSport AI," an AI-powered sports
biomechanics app for athletes.

CONCEPT: Combine two ideas into one clean icon —
1. "Drishti" means vision/sight/insight — represented by a stylized open eye or
   lens shape.
2. The product tracks athletes' body joints in real time using AI pose
   estimation — represented by a small skeletal joint-node motif: a few connected
   dots and thin lines suggesting a tracked limb or joint (like a simplified
   elbow/shoulder tracking point), integrated INSIDE or overlapping the eye/lens
   shape — for example, the pupil of the eye is a single glowing dot connected by
   one or two thin lines to two smaller dots, evoking a motion-capture joint
   marker without looking like a random circuit diagram.

STYLE: Flat, minimal, vector-style icon logo — no gradients, no photorealism, no
3D bevels, no drop shadows baked into the artwork itself. Clean geometric shapes,
confident thick linework (not thin hairlines) so the mark still reads clearly
when shrunk down to a small corner-stamp size (about 1 inch / 2.5cm wide).

LAYOUT: Icon centered above the wordmark, stacked vertically:
- Top: the eye + joint-node icon mark, inside or without a simple rounded-square
  or circular badge container
- Below: the wordmark "DRISHTISPORT AI" in a clean, bold, modern sans-serif
  (geometric sans, similar to Poppins/Montserrat Bold), all-caps, tight letter
  spacing, single line if possible (or "DRISHTISPORT" on one line and a smaller
  "AI" beneath/beside it if that reads cleaner)

COLOR PALETTE (match these exactly, this is the brand's established palette):
- Deep graphite/charcoal: #1B1F2B
- Electric amber-coral: #FF5A36
- Cyber cyan: #00C2C2
Use the icon in amber-coral or cyan (whichever reads with more energy against the
eye shape), wordmark in deep graphite. Do not introduce new colors outside this
three-color set.

DELIVER TWO VERSIONS, both on a fully transparent background:
1. Full-color version (icon in amber-coral/cyan, wordmark in deep graphite) — for
   placement on white/light slide backgrounds.
2. Pure white monochrome version (entire mark in solid white, no color) — for
   placement on dark graphite slide backgrounds.

FORMAT: Square canvas, logo mark centered with even padding on all sides, high
resolution, transparent PNG background, no mockup, no shadow, no surrounding
frame or extra text beyond the wordmark specified.

AVOID: no generic circuit-board patterns, no running-shoe or trophy clip-art
cliches, no stock "AI brain" imagery, no thin lines that would disappear at small
size, no realistic eyeball/eyelash detail — keep the eye shape abstract and
geometric.
```

---

## After you get the output

- Test both versions shrunk down to the actual stamp size (roughly 1″ square) before finalizing — if the joint-node detail turns into a blur, ask the AI tool to simplify/thicken the linework further.
- Keep consistent padding when you place it — same corner offset and same size on every slide, so it reads as a stamp/watermark rather than a shifting element.
- If the first output leans too literal (an actual eyeball) or too abstract (unrecognizable blob), you can iterate with a follow-up like: *"Make the eye shape more geometric and less anatomical, and make the joint-node dots more visible against it."*
