## Delivery notes

- **Total spoken word count target: ~485-520 words** across the ~220 seconds of actual narration (240s total minus the ~20s demo-clip insert where you go quiet) — a natural, unhurried pace. Time yourself reading it aloud before recording; trim mid-sentence rather than speeding up if you're running over.
- **The 20-second demo clip insert is the single highest-value moment in the video** — it's pre-recorded and edited in, not performed live, so there's no risk of it glitching during your actual take. If you're tight on time anywhere else, protect this segment first; don't shorten it to claw back seconds elsewhere.
- **Recording order that actually works**: (1) finish the app demo recordings and get `final_demo.mp4` / `final_demo_illegal.mp4` first, (2) edit those into the ~20s insert described above, (3) record your own narration to picture afterward, so your Solution-slide timing naturally lines up with where the clip starts and ends.
- **QR code/demo link**: visible on screen for the entire Team/Closing 15s beat, not just a flash at the very end — a judge glancing at their second screen needs a couple of seconds to actually scan it.
- Read Problem and Solution's inspiration/narration beats with the most energy — those are your two highest-weighted timing slots (45s each) for a reason.
